The game in 30 secondsA chaotic survival game: your hero attacks on its own, you just move.

You and up to three other players are dropped in the same forest. Waves of enemies keep coming. Your weapons fire by themselves — your job is to move: dodge, collect the glowing gems the enemies drop, level up and build the strongest team you can.

🎯 Goal

Survive until 30:00. If at least one of you is still alive when the clock hits 30 minutes, the whole team wins.

👥 Up to 4 players

Play online with random players or friends. Alone? You simply play solo in your room until someone joins.

🏆 Ranking

When the match ends you see everyone's kills, damage and level. In Play for prize, whoever has the most kills wins the prize.

Level up & weaponsEveryone grows on their own.

💎 Gems = experience

Enemies drop gems. Walk over them (a bigger gem is worth more) to fill your blue bar at the top. 30% of every gem you pick up is also shared with your teammates, so sticking together helps everybody.

🃏 Level-up cards

When you level up, three cards appear in the corner: new weapons or passive items. The game never pauses for anyone. If you don't pick within 15 seconds, the game picks one for you.

⚔ Weapons

Whip, Magic Wand, Knife, Axe, Cross, King Bible, Fire Wand, Garlic, Lightning Ring, Santa Water, Runetracer, Peachone, Ebony Wings, Song of Mana, Pentagram, Laurel and more. You can hold up to 6 weapons and 6 passive items (Spinach, Hollow Heart, Wings, Crown, Clover…).

✨ Evolutions & chests

Bosses drop treasure chests. A maxed-out weapon plus the right passive item evolves into a much stronger version when you open a chest.

Enemies & bosses

Red ring = enemy.   Green ring = a player (you or a friend). You always have a small gold arrow above your head, and your friends have their nickname above theirs.

Enemies come in bigger and bigger waves, with bat swarms and walls of flowers. They chase whoever is closest to them.

Every 5 minutes the screen darkens, a WARNING appears and all normal enemies vanish: a single, tough boss takes over with its own attacks (dashes, projectiles, shockwaves). Kill it to get chests and gems. Its health follows your team's damage, so it is always a real fight. If you join during a boss fight, you wait and enter when it ends.

Survive 30:00 = victory. At 30:00 Death arrives and the match ends with a victory screen and the ranking. If every player falls before that, it's game over.

Playing togetherFallen? Your friends can bring you back.

🪦 Falling & reviving

When you run out of health you leave a gravestone and watch a friend. A living friend who stays close to the gravestone for 5 seconds brings you back (a progress bar shows it; it drops if they step away). You return with half health, 10 seconds of protection, and your level, weapons and items. There is no limit on how many times you can be revived.

🚪 Joining a match in progress

You can join a normal match that's already running. To keep it fair, you start with a head start (about 60% of your friends' average level, with weapons and items picked for you), double experience until you catch up, and a few seconds of protection. You appear next to your friends.

⚖ Balance

More players means more enemies and tougher ones (about +60% enemies and +25% health for each extra player) so the match stays a challenge for the whole team.

🔒 Fair play

The server runs the whole match: damage, experience and kills are counted there, never on your computer. Nicknames are filtered, and only the ready-made chat phrases exist.

Play for prizeReal players only. Whoever kills the most enemies wins a card pack. Everyone shares the cost.

In one sentence

You and the other players split the price of one card pack; whoever gets the most kills by the end of the match wins it, and the card is sent straight to their wallet.

Step by step

1
Choose “Play for prize” and connect your walletYou need a Solana wallet such as Phantom. Connecting only shares your public address — the game never sees your secret keys.
2
Join a lobby — it's freeNothing is charged when you join. The lobby waits for at least 2 players (up to 4). No one can join once the match has started.
3
The lobby locks and the price is splitWhen there are enough players, you get 20 seconds for more to join (more players = cheaper for everyone). If nobody else shows up in 90 seconds the lobby is cancelled. Then it locks and the pack price is divided between all players.
4
Everyone pays their share in USDCYou have 60 seconds to approve one payment in your wallet. You pay in USDC (a dollar-pegged coin) and you don't need any SOL — the game covers the network fee. If someone doesn't pay in time, they're removed and everybody who already paid is refunded; the price is then recalculated.
5
Play the matchSame game, same controls, only real players. The match lasts until 30:00 or until everybody has fallen. Fallen players can still be revived by teammates, so teamwork still matters — but the ranking is about kills.
6
The player with the most kills winsWhen the match ends the ranking appears. The player with the most kills wins the pack (tie → most damage). The game buys it and the card is delivered directly to the winner's wallet — usually within seconds.

What it costs

The prize is a Starter Pack from Collector Crypt (price $25). The total is split equally, rounded up to the next cent:

The price is fixed at the moment the lobby locks. Joining the lobby is always free.

What you can win

Packs hold one random card, drawn with a provably fair random roll (verifiable on Collector Crypt). Most cards are common; some are rare and worth much more.

Important: the card is random, so it can be worth more or less than what the pack costs. Winning is never a guaranteed profit.
